P.each Peach: Materializing Immortality Desire through Data-Driven Sculptures

Yihan Wang, Yixiong Wang, Jia-Qi Shi

P.each Peach explores the intersection of data physicalization, cultural folklore, and contemporary desires for longevity. By transforming algorithmic life-expectancy datasets and bio-telemetric feeds into a series of dynamic physical sculptures reminiscent of the mythological immortal peach, the project investigates how computational artifacts can evoke visceral reflections on mortality, desire, and digital preservation. The sculptures materialize shifting health data through responsive kinetic forms and tactile surfaces, inviting audiences to reconsider the material boundaries between biological transience and digital immortality.
