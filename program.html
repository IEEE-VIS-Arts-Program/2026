Program

The VISAP 2026 programme brings together artists, designers, and researchers exploring creative, critical, and experimental approaches to data visualisation through paper and pictorial presentations. The sessions will take place on Wednesday, November 11, from 08:00 to 14:30 (US/Eastern), in Hall St. George A–B at The Westin Copley Place, Boston. Alongside the presentations, the VISAP 2026 exhibition, Amplification, will open on November 11 at 19:00 (US/Eastern) at the MIT Met Warehouse in Cambridge, featuring a keynote by Richard The. The exhibition will remain open through November 13, alongside IEEE VIS 2026. Explore the contributions to discover the papers, pictorials, and artworks featured in this year's edition.

PAPER SESSION 1

Making Data Tangible: Materiality, Space, and Public Life

Hall St. George A–B, The Westin Copley Place, Boston
Wednesday, Nov 11 @ 08:00 - 09:30 (US/Eastern)

This session brings together data sculptures, participatory performance, spatial inequality, tapestries, and urban soundscapes. The common thread is how data becomes a physical, spatial, or collective experience.
